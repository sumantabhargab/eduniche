## CONTEXT YOU NEED TO KNOW

PadhaiShuru (formerly EduNeuro) is an AI-native study orchestration platform for Indian students. It solves two problems at once: students learn through an intelligent AI system, and teachers/creators earn fair revenue through a two-sided marketplace. The domain is padhaishuru.com. The founder is Sumanta Bhargab, a student researcher in AI. The platform is in open beta with 500+ sign-ups, 50+ DAU, 5,000+ page views, 3.8 lakh+ organic reach, and 140 willingness-to-pay respondents. Zero paying users (monetisation built but not yet activated). Applying for a grant from Assam Innovation and Startup Foundation. The pitch deck must be a self-contained HTML file (or PPTX) that opens in a browser, with a near-black background, electric blue accent, Inter font, full-bleed slides, generous whitespace, keyboard/touch navigation, and subtle animations.

Brand rules:
- Brand name is PadhaiShuru. Do not use EduNeuro anywhere in the deck.
- The tagline is "The AI Study Companion" or better: "Where students learn and teachers earn."
- Tone: confident but honest, warm not hypey, specific not vague.
- No em dashes anywhere in any text. Use periods, commas, or line breaks for pauses.
- All market numbers must be in INR (rupees) where applicable, with USD equivalents in parentheses.
- The teacher/creator marketplace is not a future feature slide. It is the business model, woven through every relevant slide.
