# PadhaiShuru.com — User Personas & Journey Map

**Research Context:** GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering) preparation market in India. ~6–8 lakh aspirants register annually; ~1 lakh appear per paper. Deep segmentation exists along employment status, college tier, financial capacity, and psychological readiness. These personas are synthesized from observable behavioral patterns across Quora threads, Reddit (r/GATE), YouTube comment demographics, and coaching institute enrollment data.

# User Journey Map

## From Discovery to Premium Subscriber

### Stage 1: AWARENESS

**Touchpoints:** YouTube ad on GATE-related content, Instagram Reel from a friend, WhatsApp group share, Quora/Reddit answer mentioning PadhaiShuru, Google search ("free GATE test series")

**User State by Persona:**

| Persona | Trigger | Mindset |
|---|---|---|
| Neha (Fresher) | Stumbles on a YouTube Short: "GATE 2027 — Complete Roadmap for CS" | *"Hmm, maybe I should look into this..."* |
| Arjun (Working Pro) | Sees a Reddit post: "Best self-paced GATE resources for working professionals" | *"Finally, something that might actually fit my schedule."* |
| Rohit (Dropper) | A Telegram group member shares a PadhaiShuru test link | *"One more test series? Let me see what this one offers."* |
| Priya (Final Year) | Senior mentions it in a college WhatsApp group | *"Hope it's affordable... let me check."* |
| Vikram (Institute Student) | Fellow Made Easy batchmate recommends it | *"Supplementary practice — worth exploring."* |

**Emotional Arc:** Curiosity → Mild interest → Skepticism (*"Is this any different from Unacademy/Gradeup?"*)

---

### Stage 2: EXPLORATION (Landing Page)

**Touchpoints:** PadhaiShuru.com homepage, app store listing

**User Actions:**
- Lands on the site (often on mobile — 70% of traffic)
- Scans the hero section: "Free GATE Preparation — Structured. Personalised. Proven."
- Clicks "Take a Free Diagnostic Test" or browses subject pages
- Checks pricing — is it affordable?
- Looks for social proof: testimonials, rank stories, YouTube reviews

**Decisive Factors:**

| Persona | What Sells Them on Landing |
|---|---|
| Neha | "Free diagnostic test" — low-risk entry point |
| Arjun | "Self-paced, mobile-friendly" — fits his commute lifestyle |
| Rohit | "Previous year solved papers" — practical, immediately useful |
| Priya | "Affordable plans starting ₹199" — within budget |
| Vikram | "10,000+ practice questions" — quantity + depth |

**Drop-off risks at this stage:**
- Slow page load (they bounce — patience is ~3 seconds)
- Confusing navigation (where's the free content?)
- Pricing shock (even ₹500 is too high for some)

**Conversion to next stage:** ~30–40% of visitors take a free action (test, download, browse subjects)

**Emotional Arc:** Interest → Validation (*"This actually looks useful"*) → First commitment (free action)

### Stage 3: FIRST EXPERIENCE (Aha Moment)

**Touchpoints:** Diagnostic test, first topic module, free PYQ solutions

**User Actions:**
- **Neha:** Takes the 20-question diagnostic. Gets her estimated score: "You'd score ~62 marks with 10 months of structured prep. Here's your personalized roadmap." — **Aha!** A personalized, data-backed plan for free.
- **Arjun:** Watches his first 15-minute concept video on "Transmission Lines." Notices the explanation is clearer than the YouTube lectures he's been juggling. The video has embedded checkpoint questions. — **Aha!** This is structured, not just another playlist.
- **Rohit:** Solves 10 free PYQs from Mechanical Engineering. Each has a video solution that walks through the problem step by step, pointing out common mistakes. — **Aha!** The explanations are better than the ₹1.2 lakh coaching's answer keys.
- **Priya:** Accesses the topic-weightage analytics page. Sees that "Computer Networks" has 12–15 marks weightage every year and uses a specific question pattern. — **Aha!** Now she knows where to focus her limited time.
- **Vikram:** Takes a 25-question topic test on Power Systems. Gets a detailed report: "You scored 18/25. Weakness: Per Unit System calculations (4 errors). Suggested micro-topics to review." — **Aha!** Granular analytics his coaching doesn't provide.

**The Aha Moment Window:** Must happen within the first 15 minutes of the first session. If the user hasn't experienced a "this is different / this is useful" moment by then, they won't return.

**Emotional Arc:** Engagement → Delight → Trust building

### Stage 6: RETENTION & ADVOCACY (Premium User)

**Touchpoints:** Daily app use, weekly mock tests, community participation, achievement milestones

**Duration:** Ongoing (6–12 months until exam)

**Retention Strategies:**

1. **Personalized weekly plans** — "Your Power Systems test showed weakness in Per Unit. Here's a 3-day focused revision plan."
2. **Adaptive difficulty** — questions get harder as the user improves; prevents boredom and complacency
3. **Mock test rituals** — Sunday full-length mocks with national rankings; post-test analysis is the highlight of the week for Rohit and Vikram
4. **Community milestones** — "50 users from PadhaiShuru scored AIR under 1,000 this year!"
5. **Exam-countdown urgency** — 60 days before GATE: push intensifies (daily revision schedules, last-minute crash courses, motivational content)
6. **Parent engagement** — for younger users (Neha, Priya), weekly progress email to parents increases stickiness and willingness to renew

**Churn risks:**
- Exam over → users leave (expected; 70% churn in April–May is natural)
- Competing platforms launch aggressive discounts
- Content quality drops (stale PYQs, error-ridden solutions)
- User achieves goal (Vikram gets his rank → unsubscribes — but becomes an advocate)

**Advocacy triggers:**
- Scorecard sharing: "I scored AIR 247 with PadhaiShuru" → shareable graphic
- Referral rewards: "Refer a friend, both get 1 month free"
- Success stories featured on platform → user feels part of a community

**Emotional Arc:** Commitment → Investment → Progress → Pride → (Goal achieved → Advocacy)

```
  │  2–6 weeks of consistent free-tier engagement
  ▼
CONVERSION (480–960 — 10–20% of engaged users pay)
  │  Paywall encounter + value justification
  ▼
PREMIUM RETENTION (300–600 — 60–70% of paid users stay >3 months)
  │  Weekly mocks, analytics, community, exam countdown
  ▼
ADVOCACY (success stories → awareness for next cycle)
```

**Key Insight:** The biggest leak in the funnel is **Stage 2 → 3** (Exploration → First Experience). A slow, confusing, or unrewarding first interaction loses ~60% of interested users. PadhaiShuru's highest-impact investment is optimizing the onboarding flow and making the free-tier aha moment happen within 5 minutes, not 30.
