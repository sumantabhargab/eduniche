## Why This Distribution?

### Machine Learning (11 marks)
- **Evaluation Metrics** (F1, precision, recall) — increasingly tested as ML becomes mainstream
- **Neural Networks** (parameter counting, activation functions) — CS/EE crossover, very common
- **Clustering & Dimensionality Reduction** — unsupervised learning questions
- **Analysis basis**: ML/AI topics have grown from ~2% (pre-2020) to ~5% (2024-2025) of marks

### Information Security (6 marks)
- **Cryptography** (RSA, AES basics) — always appears
- **Authentication factors** — basic but frequently tested
- **Analysis basis**: Security contributes ~6-8% of marks, relatively stable

### Computer Vision (5 marks)
- **Edge Detection** (Sobel, Canny) — image processing fundamentals
- **Object Detection** (R-CNN, YOLO) — emerging topic, growing importance
- **Analysis basis**: CV has been introduced recently (~2022+) and shows upward trend

### NLP (5 marks)
- **Bag-of-Words & TF-IDF** — basic text representation
- **Language Models** — emerging, may appear as conceptual questions
- **Analysis basis**: NLP is new to GATE CS syllabus (added ~2023), low but growing

### Professional Ethics (3 marks)
- **IPR types** — definitions are easy 1-mark questions
- **Privacy & Cyber Laws** — conceptual awareness
- **Analysis basis**: Ethics contributes ~2-3% consistently
