### 4. Startup Profile

**Sector**: EdTech / AI-Native Education Technology

**Problem Statement**:

The problem is real on both sides of the equation.

On the student side, anyone preparing for an exam or learning a new skill ends up using five or more disconnected apps. A video platform for concepts, a question bank for practice, a study planner, a focus timer, and increasingly, an AI chatbot for doubts. None of these tools talk to each other. None of them actually know the student. The result is that students spend more mental energy deciding what to study next than actually studying. They can't tell where they're weak. They have no idea if their effort is making a difference. Over one crore engineering aspirants register for competitive exams in India every year, and four crore plus higher education students are trying to learn without any system that understands them as an individual.

On the teacher side, there are millions of skilled people in India who could teach. Engineers who understand their subjects deeply. Coders who can explain programming. Musicians, fitness coaches, data scientists, civil servants. But the existing platforms make it nearly impossible for them to earn fairly. Unacademy takes thirty to seventy percent of what a teacher earns. Other platforms lock teachers into employment contracts or demand that they build an audience from nothing. This pushes skilled people away from education and denies students access to diverse, affordable teachers. A mechanical engineer from a small town in Assam should be able to teach GATE preparation to students across India and earn a living wage, without having to move to a metro city or surrender most of their income to a platform.

PadhaiShuru exists to fix both sides of this problem at the same time.

**Solution**:

PadhaiShuru is a two-sided learning platform built around an AI layer that connects students and teachers intelligently.

For students, the platform offers everything needed to learn effectively. An AI doubt engine that answers questions with context-aware explanations. A searchable library of previous year questions with AI-generated solutions. Adaptive study plans that adjust based on how the student is actually performing. Mistake banks, performance heatmaps, and predicted papers. A 3D virtual library where students can study together in real time. And the freemium model means no card is required to start. Five free AI doubts per day, forever. Students experience real value before any payment is ever requested.

For teachers and creators, PadhaiShuru provides the infrastructure to teach any subject or skill and keep the majority of what they earn. The platform provides the audience, AI teaching tools, payment processing, hosting, and analytics. Teachers focus on teaching. The platform handles everything else. A GATE instructor can upload a course and start earning. A coder can run live coding sessions. A musician can teach production. The revenue share is structured so teachers keep seventy to eighty percent of their earnings. This is not an afterthought. It is the economic model that drives the entire platform.

The long-term vision is to expand beyond GATE into every subject, every skill, every exam. PadhaiShuru aims to become the place where anyone can learn anything, and anyone who knows something can teach it and earn.

**Innovation**:

PadhaiShuru's approach is architectural rather than incremental. Six pillars make it different:

1. AI is the product, not the content. Competitors like BYJU'S and Unacademy treat recorded lectures and teacher headcount as their core assets. PadhaiShuru treats AI as the product. The Groq-powered doubt engine with RAG, personalized study plans, and adaptive practice mean that content is just context the AI reasons over. The marginal cost of AI personalization is close to zero. The marginal cost of adding another teacher is not. This lets the platform scale personalized learning without scaling costs proportionally.

2. The platform is a two-sided marketplace from the ground up. Every feature serves both students and teachers. Students get AI-augmented learning. Teachers get AI-powered tools like auto-generated quizzes, doubt triage, and performance analytics for their classes. More teachers attract more students. More students attract more teachers. The network effects compound over time.

3. No card is required to start. Competitors use free trials that demand credit card details and silently convert to paid subscriptions. PadhaiShuru gives students real value for free, indefinitely. Five AI doubts per day, open access to PYQs, study planning tools. Payment only happens when a student chooses to upgrade. Trust has to be earned, and this model earns it.

4. PYQs are public data, and the platform treats them that way. Instead of gating previous year questions behind paywalls or watermarking public exam papers, PadhaiShuru centralizes them, normalizes them, makes them searchable, and augments them with AI. Students trust the platform because it does not exploit them. Content becomes a distribution channel rather than a locked asset.

5. Teacher economics are structured for fairness. Unacademy takes thirty to seventy percent commission. BYJU'S treats teachers as employees. PadhaiShuru lets teachers keep seventy to eighty percent. The platform provides the audience, the tools, the payments, and the hosting. Teachers bring expertise. This fairness is the single most powerful tool for acquiring quality creators, and quality creators are what bring students to the platform. Incumbents cannot copy this easily because their revenue models depend on high commission rates.

6. The 3D virtual library creates an experience that students remember. Competitors offer flat interfaces and scrollable content. PadhaiShuru's virtual library lets students walk into a space, browse shelves, study in rooms with ambient focus music, and see other people studying alongside them in real time. This is not a gimmick. Emotional engagement drives retention, and retention drives word-of-mouth. Building something like this requires genuine investment in game design and 3D engineering. It is not something that can be added as a feature later.

The technology stack includes Next.js 16, Supabase for database and real-time features, Groq for AI inference, Tailwind CSS, Razorpay for payments, Capacitor for the Android app, and various libraries for PDFs, charts, and math rendering. The AI uses RAG to ground its responses in actual platform content. The learner model is stored with privacy built in from the start.

**Current Stage**: Prototype/MVP with a live product. Five hundred plus sign-ups, fifty plus daily active users, a working AI doubt engine, a PYQ library spanning twenty GATE branches with seven hundred ninety-seven questions, a multiplayer virtual library, Razorpay payments, and an Android app. The teacher marketplace is designed and ready to build once funding is available.

**IP / Patent Status**: No patents filed yet. The platform architecture, learner model, marketplace logic, and virtual library are held as proprietary trade secrets. No IP conflicts identified.

### 5. Market & Validation

**Target Customer**:

Two groups of people.

Students, primarily GATE aspirants in India. Final year engineering students, working professionals, people who have dropped a year to prepare. Aged twenty one to twenty six, comfortable with digital tools, and motivated by the high stakes of the exam. Over time, the platform will serve JEE, NEET, UPSC, and CAT aspirants, along with university students and people learning co-curricular skills like music, coding, and fitness.

Teachers and creators, the other half of the marketplace. Engineers who can teach technical subjects. Coders who can run programming sessions. Musicians, fitness coaches, data scientists, designers, civil servants. Skilled professionals across every industry who currently lack a fair way to earn from their knowledge. The addressable pool in India numbers in the crores.

**Market Size**:

India's edtech market is worth **₹8.7 lakh crore** today and is projected to reach **₹24 lakh crore** by 2030, growing at twenty two percent per year. India is the second largest edtech market in the world, with over four thousand five hundred startups, more than one crore active learners, and seventy six crore smartphone users.

Beyond the domestic market, the global online tutoring market alone is expected to reach one hundred seventy eight billion dollars by 2030, with India as a key growth driver.

PadhaiShuru's serviceable market covers three segments totaling approximately **₹3 lakh crore**:

Test preparation across all major exams: approximately **₹1.25 lakh crore**

Higher education and college learning: approximately **₹67,000 crore**

Upskilling and professional learning: approximately **₹1.08 lakh crore**

The teacher marketplace unlocks the upskilling segment most aggressively, because it taps into India's vast pool of skilled professionals who can monetize expertise across virtually any domain.

Here is the growth trajectory PadhaiShuru is targeting:

Year one: fifty thousand students, fifty teachers, five thousand paying students, twelve lakh rupees ARR

Year three: eight lakh students, two thousand teachers, one lakh paying students, eight crore rupees ARR

Year five: fifty lakh students, twenty five thousand teachers, five lakh paying students, fifty crore rupees ARR

This is two to five percent of the serviceable market. It is aggressive but realistic given the AI differentiation, the freemium acquisition model, and the network effects of the teacher marketplace.

**Competitors**:

Unacademy, with thirty to seventy percent teacher commission and expensive plans for students. BYJU'S, massive but dealing with high costs and a trust deficit. Vedantu, focused on K-12 live classes with a narrow scope. Gradeup and Testbook, practice platforms with limited AI. PhysicsWallah, affordable but primarily a YouTube channel with limited platform features. Khanmigo and various ChatGPT wrappers, which are reactive rather than proactive. YouTube itself, which is free but unstructured and offers no monetization path for new creators.

PadhaiShuru is the only platform trying to serve both students and teachers well at the same time. Every competitor optimizes for one side. Unacademy squeezes teachers to subsidize student prices. BYJU'S employs teachers as wage labor. YouTube leaves creators to build audiences from scratch with no support. PadhaiShuru's six structural advantages make it different: AI-native architecture with near-zero marginal cost for personalization, a freemium model that builds trust before asking for payment, open PYQ content as a distribution channel, neuroscience-informed learning design, fair teacher economics at seventy to eighty percent revenue share, and the 3D virtual library as an experience that competitors cannot quickly replicate.

**Validation**:

Five hundred plus student sign-ups with fifty plus daily active users during open beta. Approximately four hundred peak daily users during the launch period. Three point eight lakh plus organic social media reach. One hundred forty willingness-to-pay survey respondents with a range of one hundred to five hundred rupees per month. One hundred twenty six platform interactions from survey responses. The product is live and functional at padhaishuru.com.

Five detailed user personas have been validated through research: a working professional preparing alongside a job, a final year student on a tight budget, a repeater who dropped a year and needs structured guidance, a fresher who is still exploring whether GATE is right for her, and a coaching institute student who wants supplementary practice and national benchmarking.

Teachers have expressed interest in preliminary conversations. Several subject matter experts have indicated they would join the platform as creators once the marketplace is live.

**Traction**:

Five hundred five website visitors cumulatively, over five thousand page views, approximately four hundred peak daily users, three point eight lakh organic reach. Twenty plus GATE branches mapped in the platform, seven hundred ninety seven PYQs ingested, AI doubt engine operational with Groq and RAG, virtual library live with multiplayer support, Android app packaged. One hundred forty willingness-to-pay respondents showing strong demand. Zero paying users so far because monetisation features are built but not yet activated. The teacher marketplace module is designed and ready for development.

### 8. Impact

**No. of Jobs Created/Proposed**:

Direct jobs: three to five in year one, including a full stack developer, a content creator, and a community manager.

Teacher earnings: fifty plus teachers earning five thousand to fifty thousand rupees per month through the platform in year one. Two thousand plus teachers earning ten thousand to one lakh rupees per month in year three. These are not platform jobs. These are skilled professionals running their own teaching businesses through PadhaiShuru. A mechanical engineer in Jorhat can teach students across India. A coder in Guwahati can run live programming sessions. A musician can teach production. The platform removes the geographical constraint on earning.

Indirect jobs: ten plus in year two through content production partnerships, expanded teacher support, and platform operations.

The key point is that PadhaiShuru does not just create a handful of platform jobs. It creates income opportunities for thousands of skilled people across every industry who currently lack a fair channel to monetize what they know. Each teacher who joins the platform creates their own employment while simultaneously expanding the platform's content library and reach to more students.

**Social/Economic Impact**:

On the student side, PadhaiShuru makes quality learning accessible to people who cannot afford expensive coaching. A typical test prep course costs fifty thousand to one and a half lakh rupees. PadhaiShuru's free tier gives students real value with no cost. The paid plans are a fraction of traditional coaching fees. Students in Tier two and Tier three cities, who do not have access to premier coaching institutes, get the same AI-powered guidance as someone in Delhi or Mumbai. Better exam outcomes lead to better postgraduate education, better jobs, and better economic mobility.

On the teacher side, the impact is about dignity and fairness. Skilled professionals in smaller cities and rural areas often have to migrate to metros or abandon teaching altogether because existing platforms extract too much commission or offer no support. PadhaiShuru lets them teach from where they are, keep most of what they earn, and reach students across the country. This is especially meaningful for Assam. Instead of losing skilled teachers to larger cities, the platform lets them serve a national audience from home.

The freemium model ensures that no student is excluded because they cannot pay. And the teacher revenue share ensures that no skilled person is excluded because they do not have a metro address or a marketing budget.

**Alignment with Assam's Priority Sector**:

Education Technology is identified as a priority sector under the Assam Incubation and Startup Policy 2025-2030. PadhaiShuru aligns in several concrete ways. It builds an AI-native education platform that improves learning outcomes for students in Assam and across India. It creates direct technology employment in Assam. It gives Assam-based teachers and skilled professionals a national platform to earn sustainable income, reducing the need to migrate. It strengthens the state's digital education infrastructure. It serves the large community of GATE aspirants in Assam's engineering colleges including Jorhat Engineering College and Assam Engineering College. And it creates a two-sided marketplace where both learners and earners in Assam benefit.
